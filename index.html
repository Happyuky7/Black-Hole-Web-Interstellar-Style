Black Hole Web: Gargantua from Interstellar, ray-traced in your browser

Look, a black hole. Every pixel follows a photon through the curved, spinning spacetime of a Kerr black hole, so you see real gravitational lensing, the photon ring, the lensed accretion disk and relativistic Doppler beaming. Fly around it freely, switch between the film look and a full-physics mode, in eight languages.

The physics of a black hole, step by step: every equation behind the renderer, with 27 verified scientific sources.
